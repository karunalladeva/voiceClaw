  • STOP AND ASK user to pick one before proceeding

2

Product Engineering

ID: product-engineering · blocked after: market-research

  • Write improved chapters after user selection

Expected outputs: chapter-*.md, table-of-contents.md

Creative Design

  • Cover art and chapter images (ComfyUI, one job at a time)

Creator
