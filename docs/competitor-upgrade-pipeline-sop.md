# How We Make a Better eBook From a Successful One

**Simple idea:** Find an eBook that is already selling well. Study it. Write a **new, better version**. Add pictures. Turn it into one PDF file. Send it to you.

You do **not** copy their words. You copy the **idea**, then make yours clearer, fuller, and more useful.

---

## The 4 steps (in order)

```
Step 1 → Pick a winner     (you choose)
Step 2 → Write the book
Step 3 → Make the pictures
Step 4 → Build the PDF and send it
```

**Important:** Step 2 cannot start until **you** pick which competitor book to copy the *idea* from.

---

## Who does the work?

Four helpers work one after another:

| Helper | What they do |
|--------|----------------|
| **Market Research** | Finds books that are actually selling |
| **Product Engineering** | Plans and writes your new book |
| **Creative Design** | Makes cover art and chapter images |
| **Creator** | Puts everything together into one PDF |

---

## Step 1 — Find a book worth copying (Market Research)

### Did you already name a book?

- **Yes** → Use that book. Skip the search. Go to Step 2 after you confirm.
- **No** → The helper searches places where people sell digital books (Amazon, Gumroad, Etsy, etc.).

### How do we know a book is really selling?

We look for proof:

- Good reviews and sales signs on the store page  
- **Ads that have been running for 2 weeks or more** — that means someone is paying to promote it because people buy it  

Short ads (less than a week) usually mean they are still testing. We ignore those.

### Then we stop and ask you

We show you the **top 5** books that look like the best targets.

**You pick one.** Nothing else happens until you choose.

*(If you turned on “auto-proceed,” we pick #1 for you.)*

---

## Step 2 — Write your better book (Product Engineering)

**Starts only after you picked a target in Step 1.**

### What they do

1. **Study the competitor** — What do buyers love? What do reviews complain is missing?
2. **Plan chapters** — A table of contents for *your* improved version
3. **Write every chapter** — All new text. Better explanations. Fill the gaps the original missed.

### Rules for writing

- **Do not copy** their sentences  
- **Do not repeat** the same point over and over to make it longer  
- Write clearly — like explaining to a friend  
- No fake promises (like “guaranteed money” or “cure diseases”)

### What gets saved

- Table of contents  
- One file per chapter  
- A short “why our book is better” note  

---

## Step 3 — Make it look good (Creative Design)

**Starts only after all chapters are written.**

### What they do

1. **Cover** — A few cover designs that look more modern than the competitor’s  
2. **Inside pictures** — Images for chapters so it is not just plain text wall-to-wall  

All images are saved to a folder so Step 4 can find them.

---

## Step 4 — Build the PDF and send it (Creator)

**Starts only after chapters + images are ready.**

### What they do

1. Collect all chapter files and images  
2. Put them in the right order  
3. Make one finished file: **digital-product.pdf**  
4. Send you the PDF (in chat or Telegram)

---

## Simple rules everyone must follow

| Rule | Why |
|------|-----|
| **One step at a time** | No writing until you pick the target book |
| **Your words, not theirs** | Legal and better for buyers |
| **No fluff** | Every page should teach something new |
| **Real proof only** | Do not fake review counts or ad data |
| **Save file locations** | Each helper tells the next one where files are saved |
